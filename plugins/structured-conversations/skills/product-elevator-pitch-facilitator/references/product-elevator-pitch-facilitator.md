# Product Elevator Pitch Facilitator

Facilitate collaborative pitch drafting using the standard product elevator pitch syntax.

## Use When

- a team needs to align on product positioning before writing feature or epic hypotheses
- several audience segments need to be discussed in the same workshop
- the pitch needs prompts for evidence, alternatives, and differentiators

## Output Characteristics

- seven pitch lines are visible as workshop sections
- the artifact captures audience, problem, value, alternative, and differentiator assumptions
- open questions identify what needs evidence before the pitch is reused
