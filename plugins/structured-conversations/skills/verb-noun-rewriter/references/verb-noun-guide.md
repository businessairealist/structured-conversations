# Verb-Noun Guide

## What to Look For

Look for statements that are dominated by nouns instead of actions.

Common noun-pile signals:

- roadmap management
- analytics support
- onboarding improvements
- reporting automation strategy
- customer experience enhancements

These phrases often hide what someone will actually do.

## Rewrite Heuristics

Turn the statement into:

- a clear verb
- a concrete object
- the smallest coherent unit of work

Examples:

- `reporting automation strategy` -> `automate weekly revenue reporting`
- `onboarding improvements` -> `reduce account setup friction`
- `analytics support` -> `answer product adoption questions`

## Weak Verbs to Challenge

Challenge verbs such as:

- `handle`
- `manage`
- `support`
- `improve`
- `optimize`
- `enhance`

Do not ban them blindly, but replace them when a more observable action exists.

## Useful Rewrite Questions

- What action should happen?
- What object is acted on?
- Who benefits or uses the result?
- Does the statement hide multiple actions?
- Can another skill consume this statement directly?

## When to Split

Split the statement when it contains:

- `and`
- `or`
- multiple deliverables
- multiple audiences
- both an outcome and an implementation

## Good End State

The rewrite is strong when another skill can pick it up without first asking, "What do you mean by this?"
