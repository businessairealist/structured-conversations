# Future Press Release Options

## Option 1

Best when the team wants a fuller announcement format with classic release
components.

Sections:

- Title
- Subtitle
- Introductory Paragraph
- Problem Paragraph
- Solution Paragraph
- Quote from a Leader
- How the Product or Service Works
- Quote from a Customer
- Learn More

## Option 2

Best when the team wants a concise customer-facing story with a straightforward
path from product name to call to action.

Sections:

- Heading
- Subheading
- Summary
- Problem
- Solution
- Quote from you
- How to get started
- Customer Quote
- Closing and Call to Action

## Option 3

Best when the team needs stronger internal alignment around the problem,
solution, user experience, and internal FAQ material.

Sections:

- Heading
- Subheading
- Summary
- Problem Statement
- Solution
- User Experience
- FAQ
- Internal Section
