# Experiment Signal Passfail Designer

Define how an experiment will be judged before results arrive.

## Use When

- an experiment exists but the evaluation logic is vague
- pass and fail are being discussed informally instead of recorded
- the team wants a reusable measurement artifact before a decision review

## Good Inputs

- experiment
- desired outcome
- key signals
- baseline or comparison logic
- pass, mixed, and fail thresholds

## Good Output Characteristics

- signals are explicit and reviewable
- thresholds are concrete enough to guide interpretation
- ambiguous outcomes are handled intentionally
- risks to data quality or interpretation remain visible
