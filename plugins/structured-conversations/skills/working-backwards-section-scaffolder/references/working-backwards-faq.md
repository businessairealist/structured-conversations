# Working Backwards FAQ

Use this reference when scaffolding or refining the FAQ portion of a working-backwards package.

Strong FAQ sections help teams surface assumptions before committing to build.

Recommended FAQ areas:

- customer problem and current workaround
- target audience and why they care
- differentiation versus alternatives
- launch readiness and adoption risks
- operational, support, and dependency concerns

Good FAQ prompts:

- Why will customers care about this launch?
- What problem will still remain unsolved?
- What assumptions are most likely to break?
- What would make this offering hard to adopt or support?
