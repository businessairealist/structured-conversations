# Initiative To Keyresult Aligner

Align candidate initiatives against measurable key results so strategy work can be defended and reviewed.

## Use When

- the team has candidate initiatives but unclear measurement logic
- OKRs exist but initiative linkage is weak
- an OKRI record or OKR board needs stronger justification

## Good Inputs

- objective or framing goal
- measurable key results
- candidate initiatives
- evidence, assumptions, or dependencies

## Good Output Characteristics

- each initiative shows which key result it is expected to move
- strong, partial, and weak linkage are easy to scan
- assumptions stay visible instead of masquerading as certainty
- the output is easy to reuse in an OKRI or OKR board
