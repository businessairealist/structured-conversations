# Outcome Driven Epic Writer Reference

Use this skill when the team needs an epic hypothesis statement that leadership
or decision-makers can review quickly.

Required statement fields:

- Funnel Entry Date
- Epic Name
- Epic Owner
- Epic Description
- Business Outcomes
- Product OKR
- Leading Indicators
- Nonfunctional Requirements (NFR)

The rendered statement should emphasize clarity and scanability over decorative
formatting.
