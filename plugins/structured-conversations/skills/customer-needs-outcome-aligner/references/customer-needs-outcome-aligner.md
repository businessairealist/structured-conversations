# Customer Needs Outcome Aligner

Use customer-needs and outcome linkage to keep roadmap bets honest.

## Alignment Questions

- which customer need does this bet support?
- which outcome should improve if this bet works?
- what evidence suggests this belongs in Now, Next, or Later?

## Good Alignment Characteristics

- visible need-to-outcome linkage
- weak bets remain explicit as assumptions
- the roadmap explains why work matters, not just where it sits
