# Elevator Pitch To Epic Translator

Translate product-value narrative into an epic hypothesis with outcomes, key results, indicators, and constraints.

## Use When

- a pitch is strong enough to become planning input
- the team needs to connect messaging to measurable outcomes
- an epic hypothesis should preserve constraints and validation questions

## Output Characteristics

- source pitch remains traceable
- epic hypothesis is concise and outcome-oriented
- key results and constraints are captured as planning inputs
