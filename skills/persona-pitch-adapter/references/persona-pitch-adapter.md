# Persona Pitch Adapter

Adapt one core pitch across multiple personas so teams can compare how the message shifts by audience.

## Use When

- the product story is stable but audience framing needs to vary
- teams want side-by-side persona pitch variants
- pitch reviews should compare resonance without rewriting everything

## Good Inputs

- product name
- core category, value, alternative, and differentiator framing
- persona or audience list
- optional persona-specific problem or benefit emphasis

## Good Output Characteristics

- one consistent pitch structure across personas
- audience differences are easy to scan
- the final pitch row shows how each persona version sounds in full
- the artifact is reusable in later comparison work
