# Future Press Release Facilitator

Facilitate a working-backwards session to draft a future press release collaboratively.

## Use When

- the team needs to align on a future customer outcome before committing to delivery work
- the press release is still a workshop artifact, not a final narrative
- risky launch assumptions need to be surfaced explicitly

## Output Characteristics

- draft heading, problem, solution, benefit, quote prompt, and FAQ prompts
- explicit open questions about proof and launch risk
- compatibility with downstream press-release and working-backwards package artifacts
