# Empathy Insight Extractor

Summarize a completed empathy map into the strongest pains, gains, tensions, opportunities, and implications.

## Use When

- the empathy map is complete enough to summarize
- the team needs a sharper downstream planning input
- the full board is too detailed for the next decision

## Good Inputs

- completed empathy-map artifact
- actor context
- a specific artifact id or slug when multiple empathy maps exist

## Good Output Characteristics

- preserves section traceability
- distinguishes evidence from inference
- identifies the strongest pains and opportunities
- is compact enough to feed planning work
