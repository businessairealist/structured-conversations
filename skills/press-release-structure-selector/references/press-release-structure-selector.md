# Press Release Structure Selector Reference

## Purpose

Use this reference when choosing among the supported future press release
formats.

The selector is not writing the final press release. It is choosing the most
useful structure and creating a starter scaffold for the writer.

## Selection Criteria

Choose the structure that best fits the type of clarity the team needs:

- choose the most detailed option when the team needs a fuller launch narrative
  plus quotes and next-step detail
- choose the middle option when the team needs a crisp story with a few strong
  sections and a short path to action
- choose the most internal option when the team needs a problem-solution
  narrative plus customer experience, FAQ, and internal alignment material

## Required Output

The selector output should include:

- a side-by-side comparison of all three options
- a recommendation
- a short reason for the recommendation
- a starter scaffold for the chosen option

## Image Guidance

The image example is a layout target for the selector output:

- left guide panel
- three option columns
- each column lists the section names and prompt guidance for that option

Do not add a blank template panel unless explicitly requested.
