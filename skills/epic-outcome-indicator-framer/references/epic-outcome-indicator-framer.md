# Epic Outcome Indicator Framer

Frame the measurable success layer of an epic before or during epic hypothesis drafting.

## Use When

- an epic idea exists but outcome logic is weak
- the team needs business outcomes, product OKR, and leading indicators clarified
- epic drafting should stay measurable instead of purely descriptive

## Good Inputs

- epic name or context
- business outcomes
- product OKR
- leading indicators
- evidence notes or assumptions

## Good Output Characteristics

- business outcomes are explicit and measurable
- product OKR linkage is visible
- leading indicators are distinct from end outcomes
- the output is easy to reuse in a full epic hypothesis statement
