# Outcome Initiative Horizon Planner

Place candidate initiatives into `Now`, `Next`, or `Later` using confidence, outcome linkage, and sequencing constraints.

## Use When

- the team has more candidate bets than it can do immediately
- roadmap conversations need a defensible horizon recommendation
- evidence, readiness, and dependency signals should shape prioritization

## Good Inputs

- strategic goal
- candidate initiatives
- linked outcomes or customer needs
- evidence, confidence, or experiment signals
- dependencies, blockers, or sequencing notes

## Good Output Characteristics

- each initiative gets a recommended horizon
- rationale is visible instead of implied
- outcome linkage stays attached to each recommendation
- blocked or lower-confidence work is clearly separated from `Now`
