# Acceptance Criteria Scaffolder

Convert a drafted story plus its important rules into observable acceptance criteria.

## Use When

- a story is ready for behavioral clarification
- the team needs acceptance criteria before example mapping, Gherkin, or implementation
- key rules, thresholds, or evidence checks must become explicit

## Good Inputs

- drafted story text
- feature-map rules
- example-map notes
- business thresholds or evidence requirements

## Scaffolding Workflow

1. identify the primary successful outcome
2. capture the main blocking or validation paths
3. encode material business rules
4. phrase each criterion as testable behavior
5. record unresolved rule questions separately

## Good Output Characteristics

- observable outcomes rather than implementation steps
- explicit blocking conditions
- traceability to the source story or mapping artifact
- enough clarity for downstream examples or scenarios
