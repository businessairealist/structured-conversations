# Goal Oriented Roadmap Builder

Build a horizon-based roadmap from a strategic goal, desired outcomes, and candidate bets.

## Use When

- the team has a clear goal and needs to place work into horizons
- customer and business outcomes should stay visible
- roadmap conversations should avoid date theater

## Good Inputs

- strategic goal
- candidate initiatives or bets
- desired outcomes
- customer needs
- evidence or confidence notes

## Good Output Characteristics

- clear Now, Next, and Later horizons
- visible outcome linkage
- customer-needs alignment
- explicit uncertainty and open questions
